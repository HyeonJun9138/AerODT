AeroDT operator / DTAM project (materials carry KADA and VIBUM logos)


Confirmed by the operator as their own asset on 2026-09-09, so AeroDT may publish it. Third-party marks inside the liveries (KADA, VIBUM) belong to their owners.
Changes: Unreal glTF exporter output, unmodified. Textures written as JPEG quality 85 by the exporter. Exported part by part from the blueprint, each mesh component replaced by an actor at the same world transform with the same material overrides, because exporting the blueprint actor itself writes the node tree without geometry. The body carries MI_CarPaint_White and the canopy MI_Glass_Windshield_Tinted, so the livery is the operator's own rather than a placeholder. Six internal battery components were left out: they are 85% of the source triangles and are never seen from outside. The remaining static meshes were reduced to 30% and exported from that level of detail; materials were baked to 512 px sets. Geometry was reduced to 30% of the source triangle count by the Unreal mesh reducer and exported from that level of detail. Repairs after export, none of which move a vertex: dropped 1 primitives that the reducer emptied; repaired 42 degenerate tangents; removed 4 unreferenced accessors and 4 buffer views.
